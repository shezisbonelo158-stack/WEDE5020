Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for a program?

Visit our Services page to learn about all available programs. You can submit an online application through our website or contact our Programs team directly for assistance.

What are your program fees?

Many of our programs are free or low-cost to ensure accessibility for all youth. Some specialized programs may have fees. Contact us for specific pricing information.

What is the age requirement?

Most programs serve youth aged 12-25, but specific age requirements vary by program. Please check individual program details or contact us for more information.

Can I participate in multiple programs?

Yes! Many of our participants are enrolled in multiple programs simultaneously. We'll help you find the right combination based on your goals and schedule.

Do you offer online programs?

Yes, we offer both in-person and online programs. Our flexible scheduling ensures you can participate regardless of your availability.

What support is available after completion?

Our alumni receive ongoing support including job placement assistance, networking opportunities, and access to advanced programs.
